About SaigonRide

Ho Chi Minh City's smart, eco-friendly vehicle rental network

SaigonRide is a modern shared-mobility platform designed for the bustling streets of Hồ Chí Minh City. Whether you need a quick bike ride across the neighbourhood or an e-scooter for a longer commute, our network of 20+ stations and 500+ vehicles has you covered — 24/7, no queues, no hassle. Every ride you take helps reduce traffic congestion and carbon emissions in the city.

🚲
Bikes & E-Scooters
Standard bikes from 500 VND/min and electric scooters from 1,500 VND/min. No hidden fees.
📍
City-Wide Coverage
Stations at key landmarks, universities, transit hubs and shopping centres across all districts.
🌿
Green Mobility
Zero-emission rides. Each trip contributes to our city-wide CO₂ offset tracking programme.
